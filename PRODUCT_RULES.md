# Product rules

- Adults 18+ only.
- Dating, friendship and communities are supported.
- Paid companionship is strictly non-sexual and limited to lawful public/social activities such as coffee, dining, movies, public events, sports and gaming.
- Prohibited: sexual services, escorting, solicitation, coercion, trafficking, illegal activity and off-platform payment pressure.
- Companion profiles must not expose identity documents, private contact details or home addresses.
- Buyers see only the information needed to evaluate a listing and complete their own booking.
- Companions see only minimum necessary booking/customer information.
- All pricing, cancellation terms, duration and meeting arrangements must be disclosed before confirmation.
- Any gender-based activation pricing requires legal review and must remain disabled until approved.
